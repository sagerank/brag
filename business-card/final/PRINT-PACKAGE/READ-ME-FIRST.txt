READ ME FIRST (for you, Veera)

WHAT TO SEND THE PRINTER
1. PRINT-ORDER-SHEET_give-this-to-the-printer.pdf   (or paste the .txt into WhatsApp / email)
2. 1_SEND-THIS-TO-PRINTER_SageRank-card_with-bleed.pdf   <- the actual card, 2 pages: front, back

ONLY IF THE PRINTER ASKS
- "Do you have crop marks?"  -> send file 2_ALTERNATIVE_..._with-crop-marks.pdf
- "Send it without bleed"    -> send file 3_ALTERNATIVE_..._trim-size-no-bleed.pdf (less safe: tell them it has no bleed)
- "Send an image"            -> source-files/png (600 dpi) - the PDF is always better
- "Send the vector / source" -> source-files/svg (text is outlined)

THE WORDS IN ONE MINUTE
Trim  = the final card size after cutting (3.5 x 2 in).
Bleed = a 0.125 in extra band of colour around the card that is cut off. It stops white edges. You do not need to do anything.
Safe  = text and the QR are kept 0.3 in away from the edge, so rounded corners or a slightly off cut never damage them.
See layout-guide_trim-bleed-safe.png for a picture.

BEFORE YOU APPROVE THE PRINT RUN
1. Ask for a PDF proof, and if possible one real printed sample.
2. Scan the QR code from the sample with your phone. Open https://www.sagerank.io/veer first to make sure the page works.
3. Check that the blue bar on the front and the blue bar on the back meet when you turn the card sideways.
4. Check the blue looks like your logo blue, and that the small text is sharp.

THINGS I COULD NOT DO FOR YOU
- The files are RGB. I could not make a reliable CMYK file here, so the printer converts it. The order sheet tells them how.
- I did not choose a printer, paper or quantity. The paper on the order sheet is only a suggestion.
- Your card is US size 3.5 x 2 in. If your printer's standard is 90 x 55 mm, tell me and I will re-make the files.
